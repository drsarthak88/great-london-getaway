# Credits

**The Great London Getaway** is an original game. All characters (Marmalade Jack,
Inspector Hemlock Bones and friends), models, textures, music and sound effects are
created procedurally in code for this project.

## Libraries
- [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) (MIT licence)
- [Vite](https://vite.dev/) (MIT licence)

## Public-domain material
- The Westminster Quarters chime (1793) is in the public domain.
- The Union Flag shown on the palace and Parliament is a national flag, drawn in code.
- Sherlock Holmes (mentioned on the 221B postcard) is a public-domain character by
  Arthur Conan Doyle; the game's detective, Inspector Hemlock Bones, is original.

## Landmarks and facts
Landmarks are stylised, original low-poly interpretations built in code. All adverts
on the Piccadilly screens are made up. Postcard facts were checked against public
references (official landmark websites and encyclopaedias).

## Third-party assets
None yet. Any CC0 props added later (Kenney, Quaternius) will be listed here.
